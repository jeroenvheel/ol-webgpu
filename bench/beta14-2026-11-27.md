# Memory and speed of 0.1.0-beta.14 (2026-11-27)

`npm run bench -- --layers=webgl2,webgpu` (three runs), `npm run test:memory`
and `npm run test:memory -- --big` on `main` at `a2e59bd` (0.1.0-beta.14).
AMD Radeon R9 200 Series, D3D11, Playwright Chromium, the machine of
[history-2026-11-26.md](history-2026-11-26.md), the morning after. One after
the other, with 9.5 GB of 16 GB free.

## Speed

Milliseconds, lower is better. Median of three runs, and the runs.
`stall` is new in the benchmark: the longest time the page got no frame
between adding the layer and `rendercomplete`. For beta.13 it was measured
the day before, in two runs.

| | stall | 307–312 | 76 (78 / 76 / 76) | 237–251 | 167 (167 / 170 / 162) |

- **The stall for a million points is a quarter of what it was on WebGL2**
  (310 to 76 ms) and two thirds on WebGPU (240 to 167 ms): the features go
  into the layer's batch in slices. What is left on WebGPU is the GPU
  process taking the buffers in, during which the browser shows no frame.
  For lines and polygons the batch was not what stalled, and little changed.
- **The first render is later**, by what the slices cost: 38 ms for points
  on WebGL2 (587 to 625), 10–21 ms for lines and polygons. Points on WebGPU
  are level (603 to 594). That is the price of the page staying alive
  meanwhile.
- Frames, picks and the worker stages are unchanged.

## Memory

Each scenario with `ol/Feature` and with RenderFeatures, on both backends:
12 cycles after 3 to warm up.

- 0 checks failed. After disposing everything 0 features, RenderFeatures,
  maps, layers, renderers, canvases and buffers are left in all four passes;
  the heap is 11.3–11.4 MB (WebGL2) and 12.1–12.2 MB (WebGPU).
- Heap growth per cycle is −1 to 10 KB, except a whole map made and disposed
  (41–46 KB, which is 0.6 MB once).

### A big layer

300k points, 200k lines, 50k polygons. Heap in MB:

| | WebGL2 beta.13 | WebGL2 beta.14 | WebGPU beta.13 | WebGPU beta.14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the features themselves | 610 | 610 | 610 | 610 |
| added by the layer | 398 | 235 | 233 | 235 |
| the same with RenderFeatures | 176 + 280 | 176 + 117 | 176 + 116 | 176 + 118 |
| a rebuild | 0.6 s | 0.6 s | 0.6 s | 0.6 s |

WebGL2 no longer keeps a copy of buffers of more than 32 MB (`keepBuffers`):
163 MB less, and as much as WebGPU now. The 2 MB more on WebGPU were not
looked into.

The heap does not grow over four rebuilds (0.2 MB in all) and is back at 8.8
(WebGL2) and 9.2 MB (WebGPU) after everything is dropped. The GPU process,
`ol/Feature`, MB (drawn → rebuilds 1 and 4): WebGL2 518 → 518 / 518; WebGPU
468 → 595 / 460, one upload more for a while, as before.
